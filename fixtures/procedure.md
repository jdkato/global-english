# Replace the filter

The filter traps dirt in the oil. The pump pushes oil through it. The gauge shows the pressure. A high reading means a full filter. Replace the filter at each service. The new filter comes in the kit. Keep the old seal for the record.

## Remove the old filter

1. Remove the cover and disconnect the hose.
2. You must close the inlet valve.
3. Using a wrench, turn the filter counterclockwise.
4. The filter is removed by hand when it is loose.
5. Clean the housing before installing the new filter.
6. Remove gasket.
7. Put the old filter in the waste bin that is next to the pump on the floor of the service room.
