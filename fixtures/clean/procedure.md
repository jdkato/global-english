# Replace the filter

The filter traps dirt in the oil. The pump pushes oil through it. The gauge shows the pressure.

A high reading means a full filter. Replace the filter at each service. The new filter comes in the kit.

## Remove the old filter

1. Remove the cover.
2. Disconnect the hose.
3. Close the inlet valve.
4. Turn the filter counterclockwise with a wrench.
5. Remove the filter by hand.
6. Clean the housing before you install the new filter.
7. Remove the gasket.
8. Put the old filter in the waste bin next to the pump.
